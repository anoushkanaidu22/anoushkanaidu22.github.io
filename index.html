About me

I work in New York City as a software engineer at Plaid. I graduated from Carnegie Mellon University with a Bachelor of Science in computer science and a concentration in computer graphics. I was born and raised in Boise, Idaho.

I love playing soccer, sketching people, playing the piano, listening to all kinds of music, putting together vlogs of my experiences with my friends, and making cocktails.

I support Aston Villa but I don’t keep up with them very well these days. My favorite Robin is Jason Todd, followed shortly by Tim Drake. My favorite animal is a jellyfish, and my favorite bird is the King of Saxony.

Website design

The background image is a production background painting from Batman: The Animated Series (1992). I grew up watching DC’s catalogue of animated shows and reading their comic books. I wanted to choose something meaningful yet aesthetically pleasing, so I settled on this painting of Gotham’s skyline in the night.
