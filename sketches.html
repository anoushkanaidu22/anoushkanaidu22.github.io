My art

I am a very casual sketcher. I think my biggest limitation as an artist is my lack of patience, and this is why all of my art is in the form of “sketches” on scrap paper rather than finished, inked pieces. I have an aversion to color, mostly just because I find it incredibly difficult and have not invested time into mastering it. I think it’s a whole separate craft that I haven’t taken interest in (seems very daunting…why take a perfectly decent sketch and risk destroying it in the pursuit of adding a bit of color? Lol, I'll leave it to those braver than I).

You’ll also notice my art is exclusive to physical paper and pencil. In the past, I have tried using an iPad and also those digital drawing pads that you can draw on and the lines appear on your desktop. For the former, I find the line weighting to be so unfamiliar and everything I produce looks subpar. For the latter, I honestly just couldn’t get used to the disconnect between where you’re drawing and where the lines appear. But I am also entirely aware that I did not give either of these methods a fair chance, and gave up far too soon. I feel bad about this as the digital drawing pad was a very considerate, personal birthday gift from my dad. I think if I took art more seriously, I would have tried harder, but I face a big enough barrier to begin drawing on paper as it is. It requires focus, patience, and determination. These days I am either too busy or too lazy to pour effort into art, so I produce sketches very rarely.
